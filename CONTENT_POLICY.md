# Responsible content use

TPlayer is an audiobook player and search client. It does not grant rights to
any work, and it cannot verify that every third-party listing or referenced
file is authorized.

## Use only authorized material

Use TPlayer only for material that:

- is in the public domain where you are;
- is offered under a license whose terms permit both this download and
  peer-to-peer sharing;
- was created by you or is controlled by you; or
- you have explicit authorization from the rightsholder to obtain and share in
  this manner.

Never use TPlayer to search for, open, stream, download, cache, or share a
copyrighted work without authorization. “Free to access” does not mean “free of
copyright.” Owning a physical or digital copy, or having a subscription, does
not by itself establish permission to obtain another copy from a third party or
upload it to peers.

Public-domain status, licenses, and copyright exceptions vary by jurisdiction.
Check the rights information and the law that applies where you are before
opening an item.

## Peer-to-peer behavior

TPlayer uses BitTorrent. Streaming and downloading can also upload pieces of
the material to other peers while a transfer is active, including before the
complete work has downloaded.

## Source boundaries

LibriVox, Internet Archive, and AudioBookBay are independently operated.
TPlayer does not host their listings and does not independently verify every
item's rights status.

- LibriVox describes its recordings as public domain in the United States, but
  copyright status can differ elsewhere.
- TPlayer limits Internet Archive audiobook search to LibriVox records or items
  with source-supplied rights information. Those labels are not a guarantee;
  review the individual item and its linked rights information.
- AudioBookBay is retained as an optional built-in source and is disabled by
  default. Its inclusion is an integration example, not an endorsement of its
  listings or a representation that any result is lawful to access.

Enabling an unverified tracker or first opening a direct magnet link, info hash,
or unverified tracker result requires a versioned acknowledgement. TPlayer
remembers the current acknowledgement in that browser. Afterward, each explicit
Open, Start, or Retry action authorizes only the selected resolution or torrent
registration request without another checkbox. Cancelling the first-use
acknowledgement starts no resolution or torrent registration request. A changed
policy version requires acknowledgement again.

## Project position

TPlayer is designed for public-domain, openly licensed, self-created, and
otherwise explicitly authorized audiobooks. The project does not promote
piracy, provide guidance for finding unauthorized copies, or treat ownership of
another copy as automatic permission for peer-to-peer acquisition or sharing.

This policy describes the project's intended use and is not legal advice.
